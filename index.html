About Me

I am a dedicated PhD researcher in Plasma Physics at Comenius University in Bratislava, holding an M.Tech in Infrastructure Engineering (Environmental Engineering) from IIT Jodhpur. My work merges experimental plasma physics with environmental monitoring — currently building an advanced ion mobility spectrometer for VOC detection in indoor environments.

Previously I've worked on carbonaceous aerosols (EC/OC) over semi-arid regions and contributed to EIA reports for infrastructure projects. My interdisciplinary background allows me to approach air pollution, green building performance, and low-carbon development from a unique perspective.

I regularly collaborate with atmospheric physics groups and material scientists, and I'm passionate about applying plasma technology to real-world environmental challenges.
